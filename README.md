# ARI Traffic

Phone map of nearby aircraft on the FAA sectional, with a bearing and range line from your position to each aircraft on screen.

Open the GitHub Pages link on your phone and allow location access.

- Aircraft data: [airplanes.live](https://airplanes.live) (non-commercial / internal use), refreshed every 5 seconds; adsb.lol is used if airplanes.live does not answer.
- Chart: FAA San Antonio, Houston and Brownsville sectionals, 29 Oct to 24 Dec 2026, covering ARI's operating regions only.

Awareness aid only. Not for navigation or collision avoidance. Internet ADS-B data is delayed, thin at low altitude, and does not show aircraft that are not broadcasting.
